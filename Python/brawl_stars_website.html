About Brawl Stars

Brawl Stars is a fast-paced multiplayer battle arena developed by Supercell. With quick matches lasting 2-3 minutes, unique game modes, and a diverse roster of Brawlers, it's the perfect game for instant fun on mobile devices.

  • 3v3 Team Battles: Fight in dynamic arenas with your teammates
  • Solo Showdowns: Battle against other players in free-for-all matches
  • Dynamic Gameplay: Each Brawler has unique abilities and playstyles
  • Regular Updates: New Brawlers, maps, and features added regularly

Game Modes

  • Gem Grab: Collect 10 gems to win the match
  • Showdown: Last brawler standing wins
  • Bounty: Earn stars by defeating opponents
  • Heist: Protect or steal the safe
  • Brawl Ball: Soccer-style competitive action
  • Hot Zone: Control the center zone to win
